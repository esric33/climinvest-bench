## ClimateFund

The normalized project inputs and derived benchmark references use ClimateFund,
*An Annotated Dataset of Climate Mitigation Projects for Supporting Question
Answering*, by Javier Sanz-Cruzado Puig, Miruna Clinciu, Edward Richards,
Richard McCreadie, Craig Macdonald and Iadh Ounis. The upstream dataset
declares MPL-2.0. The benchmark selects 20 projects, normalizes evidence,
introduces labelled synthetic assumptions, and derives task and evaluation
references. See the [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JavierSanzCruza/ClimateFund),
[pinned source and checksum](sources/climatefund.json) and
[MPL-2.0 license](LICENSES/MPL-2.0.txt).

First published by the [Green Climate Fund](https://greenclimate.fund). Its
[website terms](https://www.greenclimate.fund/terms-and-conditions) cover use
of original GCF materials. Source proposals are available through the project
links in ClimateFund.
